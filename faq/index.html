Frequently asked questions

Who is the course for?

Anyone interested in learning more about paediatric trauma, across the multidisciplinary team.

We assume APLS, ATLS or ETC level of experience, but completing those courses is not a requirement to attend.

What is included?

Before the course: about two weeks before, we email you a link to pre-course talks and videos.

On the days: breakfast, lunch and drinks throughout. Please give any dietary requirements on your booking form.

Afterwards: a certificate of participation is emailed to you. The course awards 12 CPD points (RCPCH, RCoA, RCEM).

What are the timings?

The course starts at 8.30am on both days.

On Day 1 we aim to finish at 6pm, and on Day 2 at 5.30pm.

What are the course fees?

£500 for two days until May 2026, increasing to £600 from November 2026.

Where is it held?

Simulation Centre, Education Academy (in the basement of the Dental Hospital), Royal London Hospital, Whitechapel, London E1 1FR.

Nearest station: Whitechapel, on the District, Hammersmith & City, Overground and Elizabeth lines. See map.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you need to cancel for any reason, please contact us to discuss: rlhsimadmin.bartshealth@nhs.net or 020 3594 6821.
